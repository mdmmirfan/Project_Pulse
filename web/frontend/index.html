What the two maps mean

Sound lens places each song by a 768-dimensional neural fingerprint of how it actually sounds. Two songs sit close if they sound similar — even across different genres.

Feature lens uses eight human-readable qualities (brightness, tempo, percussiveness, dynamics, tonality, melody-vs-drums…). It's the "why" behind the sound map.

The background cloud is 320 real, genre-labelled tracks (FMA). Notice how one genre scatters across the map: genre is a label, sound is a measurement — and they don't line up.

What your pattern says

Your taste type and acoustic signature appear here once you've added songs. It's built from real measurements of your songs compared to the whole atlas — honest, not a horoscope. (Though it'll look like one. ✨)
