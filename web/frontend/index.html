  • Cloud colour = dominant genre label on background tracks (from a research dataset).

  • Hover a cloud = genre mix + what it sounds like (warm, fast, percussive…).

How Pulse came to be

I mapped 99 of my own liked/disliked songs and found taste overlaps heavily in sound — acoustics explain part of taste, not all of it. Pulse is a public map: 320 tracks from the Free Music Archive (8 genres). Honest caveat: FMA skews Western/indie — not all of world music.

Not Spotify

Spotify recommends from what similar people stream. Pulse places songs from what the audio sounds like. Your iTunes/Apple genre tag is never used for placement.

Why genre scatters

"Rock" or "Alternative" aren't one sound — they're shelves with dozens of acoustic shapes. The map shows that scatter honestly.
